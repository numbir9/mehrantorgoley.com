About

Mehran C. Torgoley writes, directs and edits feature films, shorts, music videos and commercials. His found-footage horror Curse of Aurore opened at number one at Cinémas Guzzo in Quebec before a worldwide release; his mockumentary Izzy Lyon: The Unspun Truth followed in 2022, and Golden—an Official Selection of the 2026 NYC Independent Film Festival, where Leah Eckardt won Best Actress in a Feature Film.

Before film, he was a lead artist and cinematics producer in video games. He runs Torgo Creative, a motion graphics studio.
