About

Mehran C. Torgoley writes, directs and edits features, shorts, music videos and commercials. His found-footage horror Curse of Aurore opened at number one at Cinémas Guzzo in Quebec and was an Official Selection of the 2022 Ravenheart International Film Festival, where Llana Barron won Best Actress. The mockumentary Izzy Lyon: The Unspun Truth followed in 2022. Golden, his latest, premiered at the NYC Independent Film Festival in June 2026, where Leah Eckardt won Best Actress.

Before film, he was a lead artist and cinematics producer in video games. He runs Torgo Creative, a motion graphics studio.
