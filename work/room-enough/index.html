Room Enough

Short film — 2010

Full film

Watching a clone with his exact memories—and none of his baggage—publish his book and live his life better than he ever could, a burnt-out diner worker confronts the quiet sting of becoming obsolete in his own skin.

Written, directed and edited by Mehran C. Torgoley · Inspired by a story by Isaac Marion
